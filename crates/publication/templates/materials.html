Protocol, interpretation and limits

All 36 architectural/object roles are covered, with three floor types and a dedicated rug dye/pile program shown separately. Six fixed illustration seeds (0, 1, 4, 19, 53, 101) produce 234 native PBR views. The production material factory supplies every handle and texture. CPU reference maps, metric swatches, a fixed reflection environment, indoor exposure and fixed 1,400/250 lux studio lights isolate material appearance. This is not a throughput benchmark.

Twelve finish slots reuse one to three map structures per surface per room. Rug fields share at most four dedicated dye/pile maps. Room seeds resample the recipes. Shared maps preserve coherent finishes and bound preparation cost. Molded keyboard keys and printer shells use plastic, with charcoal, warm-white and colored pigments sharing dielectric microstructure. Painted metal is dielectric; chrome is conductive. Screens and prints use dedicated content programs. Optical constants for container glass and liquids include fixed values.

Map previews show level zero: base color precedes the material tint, roughness shows the packed green channel before its multiplier, and tangent normals differ from rendered view-space normals. A swatch cannot demonstrate photographic realism or perceptual embedding volume. The separate 256-room assignment cohort excludes humans and neighboring objects; walls, ceilings and facade privacy treatments are not part of that object denominator. Skin pigmentation and groom appearance require actor-specific qualification.
