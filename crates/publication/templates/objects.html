What does geometry coverage measure?

Six 24 × 24 orthographic depth maps describe each object's first surfaces, normalized by its fixed placement envelope. Distance averages depth differences over hit pixels; a missing surface costs one. Ordered greedy coverage counts representatives separated by a given distance. Three thresholds expose sensitivity. Color, texture and triangle counts do not increase coverage.

Counts are finite-sample diagnostics, not independent combination counts, SigLIP volume or a guarantee of learning utility. Thin details and flat content may be under-resolved. Saturated categories need a larger or finer audit. CPU build timings exclude scene placement, texture generation, uploads and rendering.

All samples pass finite mesh attributes, normal/winding, index, semantic and envelope checks. Those checks do not certify every contact, hidden self-intersection or physical construction. Room-level placement tests and the unfiltered room captures complement this isolated studio protocol.
